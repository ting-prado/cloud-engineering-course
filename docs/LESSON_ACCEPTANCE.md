# Mandatory lesson acceptance gate

Applies to every future R2 week and substantive revisions to a ready week. A week can transition from `planned` to `ready` only after **all three sessions** pass this gate and a completed evidence record is committed alongside the content. A batch deadline does not waive the gate. Passing field-presence checks, word counts, a build or browser tests is insufficient.

Create `docs/acceptance/week-NN.md` from [the acceptance template](acceptance/TEMPLATE.md). Record the exact content/fixture revision, actual reviewer, review date, environment, observations and evidence paths. Check an item only after its review or execution happened. Do not invent peer review: identify author self-review explicitly when that is the review performed. Sanitize evidence and keep assessment solutions out of the learner's pre-submission view.

## Required checks

The [instructional writing standard](INSTRUCTIONAL_WRITING.md) is part of this gate. Review all three sessions for first-use term definitions, examples, connected teaching, understandable questions and actionable instructions. Record where a student works, why, the exact commands/options, expected results and evidence. Independent tasks must stay independent. A glossary or automated readability score cannot replace that walkthrough.

## Cloud cleanup evidence is mandatory

For any lab claiming actual Azure provisioning or administration, attach a sanitized execution record showing:

1. The tested subscription/scope (redacted identifier is acceptable), lab-owned resource inventory, estimated charge sources and intended retained/shared exclusions **before deletion**.
2. The exact cleanup commands/actions, their actual results and handling of locks, dependencies or asynchronous deletion. Do not equate a deletion request being accepted with resources having disappeared.
3. A subsequent inventory confirming the targeted resources are absent. Check relevant separately managed dependencies, such as disks, public IPs, registries, storage, telemetry destinations or recovery items, when the lab creates them. Explain every remaining resource and who owns its cleanup.
4. Any intentionally retained data, state or recoverable/soft-deleted items, their retention behavior and potential continuing costs. Verify current service-specific behavior rather than assuming resource-group deletion covers every lifecycle.
5. A rerun/no-op check showing cleanup can be repeated safely, or an explicit expected already-absent result. Show that shared/unrelated resources were not targeted.

A delayed cost report is supporting evidence when available; an empty current invoice is not proof of cleanup or a guarantee against delayed charges. Never publish credentials, tokens or a secret-bearing Terraform state file as evidence.

## Simulations, blockers and release decision

A local or supplied-evidence alternative must actually run or have its supplied evidence technically validated, as appropriate. State precisely which objectives it supports and which live-cloud capabilities it cannot demonstrate. It cannot satisfy a promised cloud execution or cleanup competency. If the declared primary lab is cloud-based and cloud execution/cleanup is unverified, the week remains `planned`; alternatively, revise its objectives and cost label honestly before reviewing a genuinely local/simulated lesson.

The release decision must list every checklist result, evidence and resolved correction. Any failed, pending or unsupported required item keeps the week `planned`. An item may be not applicable only with a reason consistent with the objectives and tested lab scope; cloud cleanup is not optional for a cloud lab. Only after acceptance should the author change readiness metadata and activate assessment mappings, following the existing progress-versioning rules.

Content, commands, provider behavior, required links or fixtures changing after acceptance require review of affected gates and updated evidence before rerelease. Archive or migrate assessment changes safely. Tests are supporting reproducibility and application checks; they do not decide whether the teaching is technically sound or establish a student's independent competence.

Week 1 retains its existing Stage 1 verification record. This policy does not fabricate a new independent review or a completed acceptance checklist for it. Apply this gate when substantively revising it and to all subsequent weeks before publication as ready.
