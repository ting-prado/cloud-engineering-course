## Required acceptance checklist

- [ ] Technical explanations, diagrams, examples and command meanings reviewed against inspected sources; corrections resolved.
- [ ] Writing walkthrough completed against `docs/INSTRUCTIONAL_WRITING.md`: terms defined at first use, glossary examples, what/why/how explanations, actionable guided steps and contextual commands; independent solutions remain withheld. Evidence records actual review findings and corrections.
- [ ] Published artifact/setup and guided lab executed in the supported environment; expected outcomes observed.
- [ ] Independent variation executed from its requirements and validated, including negative/control checks.
- [ ] Deliberate fault reproduced, valid constrained solution executed, original operation retested; reviewer solution and acceptable alternatives recorded without learner answer leakage.
- [ ] Every objective mapped to appropriate practice and assessment; all keys/distractors/explanations and practical/short-answer rubrics reviewed.
- [ ] Assessment behavior verified: answers withheld until submission; objective grading correct; manual assessment honest; retakes and any ungraded diagnostic behave correctly.
- [ ] Required resources opened and assigned sections inspected; URLs, dates and selection recorded; optional access limitations labeled.
- [ ] Cost, security, permissions, inventory and maximum running window verified for the actual path; no guaranteed-free or automatic-budget-shutdown claim.
- [ ] Published cleanup executed and verified, with safe rerun/already-absent behavior and no unrelated targets.
- [ ] For actual cloud labs: pre/post inventories, deletion completion, remaining dependencies/retained data and potential continuing costs evidenced. For local-only labs: explain why cloud cleanup is not applicable.
- [ ] Reader workflow and 180/180/120-minute scope reviewed, including all required reading, setup, evidence, cleanup and remediation.
- [ ] Source/artifact versions match evidence; required software checks pass; no unresolved acceptance blockers.

For every check above, record **pass/fail/pending/not applicable, reviewer, date, evidence path and reasoning** below. A checked box without evidence does not pass.

## Review and execution evidence

### Teaching and authoritative sources

Record claims reviewed, inspected source sections, technical corrections and their verification. Include resource access dates and required reading selections.

### Actual lab runs and troubleshooting solution

Record clean setup, actual versus expected outputs, variation result, fault symptom, hypothesis, minimal repair, original-operation retest and negative/control result. Distinguish observed results from proposed or supplied evidence. Include both artifact identity and environment.

### Cost, safety and cleanup

For cloud labs attach dated estimate/assumptions, inventory and cleanup execution evidence meeting [the mandatory gate](../LESSON_ACCEPTANCE.md#cloud-cleanup-evidence-is-mandatory). Document retained items and potential costs. For local labs record stopped listeners/processes and safe artifact handling. List untested alternative paths and their learner-facing restrictions.

### Assessment and timetable review

Record key/rubric review, alignment gaps and their corrections, answer-withholding checks and actual/estimated timing per activity. Do not present a timing estimate as a student pilot.
